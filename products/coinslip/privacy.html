Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

CoinSlip is a browser-based invoice formatter. It does not require an account and does not send invoice fields to a CoinSlip server.

Information you enter

Invoice details are processed in your browser. If you choose “Save on this device”, the draft is stored in your browser's local storage. Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to view it. Clear saved browser data to remove it.

Exports and sharing

Printing, PDF creation, JSON export, CSV export, and clipboard actions are initiated by you. You control where exported files are saved and with whom they are shared.

Wallets and payments

CoinSlip does not connect to wallets, request seed phrases or private keys, custody funds, monitor blockchain transactions, or verify payment. Never treat an invoice or screenshot as proof of payment.

Third parties

When hosted by a third-party platform, that platform may process basic technical information such as IP address and request logs under its own privacy terms. CoinSlip does not operate analytics or advertising trackers in this app.

Contact

This project currently does not publish a dedicated support address. Do not submit secrets or sensitive personal information through public issue trackers.
